Current status after correcting the shared pooling confound (2026-11-18)

Accepted evidence: #594 plus the common FP32 pooling fix passes 12 pooling
checks, 73 attention checks and 29 independent-process cases. Controlled
full-forward latency improves 4.87% against #593 plus the same fix. The
independent-process attention improvement is 8.26-8.93% on two real H3 layers.
This is SM100 engineering hardware, not a production B200 measurement.

protected-q-contiguous-fp32: 73 attention checks and 29 independent-process
cases pass. Real-input prepared/output hashes also match. However, graph
latency is 0.369% slower at layer 0 and 0.030% slower at layer 25; no positive
performance result. Not adopted. No full-model timing was run for this variant.

All other old experimental statuses were measured with uncontrolled BF16
pooling. Their prior failures do not isolate an attention-kernel regression,
and their timings do not establish accepted performance. They remain archived
for diagnosis and need repaired-pooling revalidation before adoption.
