The candidate combines protected-Q route bypass with contiguous K/V TMA copies
on complete protected pairs. Eligible source-level copy issues fall from 8 to
2, retaining the original fallback and identical byte volume. Fresh compiler
inspection reports REG168, STACK0, LOCAL0 and static SHARED1024 for both sides;
constant bytes rise 2040->2424. Static PTX branch sites rise 359->378, TMA sites
14->18 because both paths remain. Static sites are NOT dynamic execution counts.
No spill, achieved-occupancy or unique runtime-bottleneck claim is made. The
extra path/control work may offset a producer saving already hidden by compute;
this remains a hypothesis requiring runtime counters.

Root cause and correction
Native and pointer-reference pooling reduced BF16 without explicit promotion.
Changing autotune reduction trees changed KC/VC, thresholds and routing; the
pointer oracle shared this upstream issue. The earlier #594 attention-regression
attribution is retracted. Freezing identical prepared tensors makes #593/#594
match on the original counterexample. Explicit FP32 accumulation fixes the
observed config variance while retaining BF16 outputs and all launch choices.
The standalone public-source reproduction observes 12 numerical failures before
and 12 passes after the fix, no errors/skips. The native 32-config sweep matches
Float64-to-BF16 exactly on all tested cases. Repaired #593/#594 pass 73 attention
checks and 29 independent-process cases. See CORRECTNESS_BLOCKER.txt for detail.
