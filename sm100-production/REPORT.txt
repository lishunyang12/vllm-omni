Actual H3 Ref2VA full single forward on 2 x SM100:
50 layers, first conditional step 0, TP1/Ulysses2, BF16 eager, official 768p/5s
request, 3 warmup rounds and 9 rotating/reversing measured rounds. Maximum-rank
synchronized wall medians: Dense 14.727 s; original SOL 16.503 s; candidate
15.764 s. Original/candidate = 1.0469x, 4.48% lower latency. Every paired round
improved (1.0420x-1.0488x); original/candidate CVs 0.463%/0.504%.
The full run requested 750 MHz but observed clock drops and power-cap flags
on both GPUs. This is a paired same-policy result, not constant-clock evidence.
The 600 MHz attempt hit the framework's 600-second request timeout near the
last measured rounds. It lacks final rank reports/output checks and is not
accepted as a performance result. Its logs are retained. A 450 MHz follow-up
is running with 1800-second wave/output deadlines. The 750 MHz run is retained.
Candidate remains 7.04% slower than Dense in this operating condition.
First and last original/candidate outputs match bitwise on both ranks.
Input hashes did not change. No sampler advancement or VAE decode is timed.

Correctness:
- 94 passed, 19 hardware-specific skips on each GPU; 41 new regression tests.
- 12 further baseline bitwise stress comparisons per GPU, including zero, tiny
  and subnormal V and changing maxima.
- CUDA graph replay with updated Q/K/V/masks, nondefault stream, and no new
  compilation; independent hybrid oracle, exact mask/sink and partial tiles.
- The first suite exposed four reference-test errors on each GPU. All four
  also reproduced on unmodified upstream: KV-only sink semantics and BF16
  subnormal output rounding. The independent oracle/tolerance was corrected
  with explicit rounding accounting and nonzero tiny-output checks; no kernel
  behavior was changed to make those tests pass. Initial and corrected logs
  plus the upstream reproduction are retained.
- Skips: 11 SM80-only, 2 SM90 split-KV, 6 MPS. No fresh SM90/SM120 hardware
  run of this commit; their dispatch/kernels are unchanged.

These are profiling failures, not unprofiled execution or correctness failures.
No new occupancy, tensor-core utilization, barrier-stall or runtime spill
claims are made from incomplete profiler runs.
